# Placement & Internship Policy

## General Eligibility for Placement Drives

A student may participate in placement drives and internship applications when
**all** of the following hold:

1. The student is in the 3rd or 4th year of study.
2. The student's CGPA is at least **7.5** (on a 10-point scale).
3. The student has **no active backlogs** (all failed courses cleared).
4. The student's attendance for the current semester is at least **75%**.

## Google Summer Internship — Eligibility Criteria

For the Google Summer Internship (see event E102), candidates must additionally meet:

1. Branch: CSE, IT or ECE.
2. Year: 3rd or 4th.
3. CGPA of at least **8.0**.
4. No active backlogs.
5. Attendance of at least **75%** for the current semester.
6. Strong proficiency in at least one programming language (e.g., Python, Java, C++).

Candidates who meet the criteria are encouraged to register for the pre-placement
workshop **"Crack the Technical Interview"** (event E101) before applying.

## Registration Rules

- Workshop registration is first-come, first-served up to venue capacity.
- A student may register for a workshop only if their year and department are in
  the event's eligible lists.
- Duplicate registrations are rejected.
