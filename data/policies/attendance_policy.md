# Attendance Policy

## Minimum Attendance Requirement

1. Every student must maintain a minimum of **75% attendance** in each course
   for the semester to be eligible for end-semester examinations.
2. Attendance is computed as:
   `attendance_pct = (classes_attended / classes_held) x 100`.

## Shortfall Handling

1. A student whose attendance falls below 75% is issued a **warning notice**
   via email at the start of the week in which the shortfall is detected.
2. Students below 75% may apply for a **makeup examination** under the
   Examination Regulations, provided the shortfall is due to a valid reason.
3. Excused absences (medical, institutional duties with prior approval) are
   not counted against the student.

## Consequences

- Attendance below 60% at semester end disqualifies the student from writing
  exams in that semester and the courses must be repeated.
- Attendance below 75% but above 60% is handled case-by-case through the
  makeup examination route.
