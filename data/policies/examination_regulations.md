# Examination Regulations

## Makeup Examination Policy

1. A student is eligible to sit for regular end-semester examinations only if their
   attendance in each subject is at least 75% for the semester.
2. Students with attendance below 75% may apply for a **makeup examination**
   through a formal written request (email) addressed to the Head of Department.
3. The application must include the reason for the shortfall (medical, emergency,
   or institutional duties) and supporting evidence where applicable.
4. Applications must be submitted **at least 3 days before** the scheduled exam.
5. The exam cell reviews applications within 2 working days and approves only
   those with a valid, verifiable reason.
6. Approved makeup exams are conducted within 15 days of the regular exam.

## General Rules

- A minimum of 40% is required in theory, and 40% in practicals, to pass a course.
- Grade point scale: A+ (10), A (9), B+ (8), B (7), C (6), D (5), F (fail, 0).
- An F grade must be cleared in the supplementary examination held after results.

## Exam Day Rules

- Students must carry their ID card to the examination hall.
- Electronic devices, notes and smart wearables are prohibited inside the hall.
